Talent is everywhere. Access isn't.

A test-prep course, a summer research lab, a math team that travels: these shape where students go next, and too many of them come with a price tag.

We exist to change who gets to take part.

  • Remove the cost

    Programs are free or low-cost, and need-based scholarships cover what remains.

  • Raise the ceiling

    Beyond catching up: research, competitions, and serious writing for students ready to go further.

  • Clear the path to college

    Applications, essays, financial aid, and the exams that open selective schools.

  • Fit around real lives

    After-school and summer schedules built for students who work, commute, or care for family.

Partnerships

We work alongside schools and community groups, not around them.

What a partner school can expect

  • Tutoring and test prep on site or nearby
  • Shared STEM, research, and competition programs
  • College-access workshops for students and families
  • Scholarship outreach to the students who need it

How we'll know it's working

Success means more students take part, learn, and move forward. As programs launch, we'll report on four things.

Who takes part

How many students join, and from which schools and neighborhoods.

How they grow

Progress in grades, test scores, and skills over time.

Where they get in

Admission to specialized high schools, research programs, and competitions.

What comes next

College applications, acceptances, and financial aid secured.
