Who We Are

Removing the barriers between students and their potential.

EEFA Foundation exists to expand access to educational opportunities for students, particularly those who face financial or other barriers to academic enrichment.

  • Expand access

    Free and low-cost tutoring, test prep, and enrichment for students who need it most.

  • Encourage excellence

    STEM, research, math competitions, and writing programs that stretch curious minds.

  • Open pathways

    College-access guidance and scholarships that turn ambition into enrollment.
